Questions before you buy

How do I get it?

Right after you pay, you get an email with your link. Watch on your phone, tablet or TV. Nothing to install.

How much time does it take?

About 10 minutes a day: one short cartoon and one small habit. Miss a day? Just pick up where you left off.

Is it a subscription?

No. The Toolkit and the 30-Day Reset are one payment, and they’re yours to keep. Only the Crew Club is monthly or yearly, and you can pause or cancel it yourself.

Can I get a refund?

You get instant access to digital content, so all sales are final. If something doesn’t work, like a link or a file that won’t open, email us and we’ll fix it fast. The Crew Club is different: pause or cancel it yourself, anytime, in 2 clicks.

Is this medical advice?

No. It’s learning for parents, built on everyday habits. We don’t diagnose, treat or promise results. If something worries you, call your pediatrician first.

Can I give it as a gift?

Yes. Send it as a gift and they get a card by email on the day you pick.
