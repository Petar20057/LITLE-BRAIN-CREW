Questions about the program

Who can join?

Only Crew Club members, monthly or yearly. The program is invite-only: ask in your account, we check your membership, and Creem emails you the invite.

How long does my link count?

60 days after someone opens it.

When do I get paid?

Creem holds each commission for 7 to 12 days, then pays out on the 1st and 15th of the month once you have $50 or more. You verify your ID once with Creem (usually 1 to 2 working days) and choose bank transfer or USDC. Creem charges a payout fee: $7 or 1% for a bank transfer, whichever is higher, or 2% for USDC.

What if I cancel my Club later?

You stay in the program and keep earning on your link. Membership is what gets you in.

Does it count if they pay to support the crew?

No. Support payments aren’t a purchase, so there’s no commission on them.
