the science behind every habit

Two rules behind every habit.

No willpower speeches. No 47-step routines. Just two things the research keeps coming back to.

Rule 1 · Plasti

The brain changes from what’s repeated.

Every bedtime at the same time and every screen ending done the same way carves the path a little deeper. Scientists call it neuroplasticity, and it’s strongest in childhood. That’s why one small habit, repeated, beats one big talk.

Rule 2 · Duo

Kids borrow your calm before they can make their own.

A calm grown-up close by helps a child’s body settle. Scientists call it co-regulation. Your kid learns to calm down by doing it with you, hundreds of times. In this crew, that’s you. You’re Duo.
